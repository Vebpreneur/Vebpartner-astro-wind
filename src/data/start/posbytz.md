## What is the opportunity?

PosBytz offers a partner model for businesses that want to sell a POS and business-management platform under their own brand. Instead of developing the core software stack from scratch, a partner can focus on positioning, distribution and the customer relationship.

## How the model works

The white-label model separates the technology layer from the commercial layer. PosBytz provides the underlying hosted platform and product updates, while the partner can present the service under its own brand, set its own pricing and manage its own customers.

## What this can enable

- Launch a branded POS or business-software offer without building the full platform internally.
- Set your own customer pricing.
- Keep the direct commercial relationship with your customers.
- Build a model designed around recurring software revenue.
- Leave hosting and platform updates to the underlying provider.

## Who may find it relevant?

The model may be relevant to SaaS operators, IT providers, digital agencies, consultants and entrepreneurs that already have — or can build — distribution to merchants and other businesses that need POS and operational software.
